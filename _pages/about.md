I'm a photonics engineer with eight years across **integrated-photonic design and characterisation, quantum hardware, high-speed / RF electronics, semiconductor verification, and lab automation**. I like problems that sit between disciplines — translating a system- or protocol-level target into optical, RF, thermal, and mechanical specs, then building the test bench that proves it.

This site is the longer story a one-page CV can't hold. The [projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) are grouped into pillars — photonics & PICs, quantum systems, test & metrology, semiconductor & VLSI verification, electronics & embedded, ML & computational, and a corner for life beyond engineering. Each entry is a short Problem → Approach → Result, and the collection grows as I add more.

Currently a Senior Photonics Engineer at Nu Quantum (Cambridge). Earlier: photonics research across ICFO, Ghent, and VUB; DRAM IP verification at Siemens EDA; and robotics electronics at Grey Orange.
