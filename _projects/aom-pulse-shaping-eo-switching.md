**Problem.** Deliver shaped optical pulses with precise timing and phase for high-fidelity control in AMO / trapped-ion experiments.

**Approach.** Modelled and controlled **phase-locked acousto-optic modulators (AOMs)** for optical pulse shaping — RF-driven electrical-to-optical control — and integrated electro-optic entanglement switching into automated ARTIQ/Python pipelines.

**Result.** Reliable shaped-pulse delivery for qubit operations; an RF↔optical control capability directly relevant to transduction and qubit-control signal delivery.
