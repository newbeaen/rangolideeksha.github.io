**Problem.** Manual characterisation across two tapeout generations was too slow and inconsistent for the data volumes needed.

**Approach.** Built **automated measurement pipelines and instrument control** in Python and ARTIQ (tunable lasers, power meters, SNSPD counters, probe station, RF drivers, AOMs); added root-cause analysis feeding back into design and roadmap.

**Result.** **>10× improvement in test throughput/speed** and reliability at wafer- and die-level.
