**Problem.** Recover high-resolution detail from low-resolution images beyond what interpolation achieves.

**Approach.** Designed a **residual-learning CNN** in PyTorch (NAdam optimizer) for 4× super-resolution.

**Result.** Outperformed interpolation baselines on the **DIV2K** benchmark.
