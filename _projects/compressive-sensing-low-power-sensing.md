**Problem.** Wearable optical sensors (e.g. PPG) burn power in both continuous sampling and the radio that ships the data — limiting battery life.

**Approach.** Applied **compressive sensing / sparse-signal recovery**: sub-sample the signal and reconstruct it, cutting both acquisition and communication load. Prototyped the recovery method and supported the low-power hardware implementation.

**Result.** ~**70% power reduction** across sensor and communication subsystems; method captured in **US Patent P95539**.

*Intel Labs. Co-inventor. Tools: MATLAB/Python, sparse recovery.*
