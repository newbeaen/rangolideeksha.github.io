**Problem.** A flagship EDA product line needed rigorous, JEDEC-compliant verification of DDR4/DDR5 DRAM IP with signal-integrity confidence.

**Approach.** Led development of **JEDEC-compliant checkers, coverage, and statistical performance characterisation** (throughput, timing, power) in **SystemVerilog, UVM and C++** across DDR2–DDR5.

**Result.** Reusable verification/analysis components adopted across the DDR line — deep test-automation and data-analysis rigour that transfers directly to DfT and driver/SerDes co-design.

*Siemens EDA (Lead Member Technical Staff).*
