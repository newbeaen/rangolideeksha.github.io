**Problem.** Free-space and multi-core-fibre quantum communication needs entangled-photon sources whose spectral properties are understood across operating conditions.

**Approach.** Studied **SPDC**-based entangled-photon sources and how their spectra shift with temperature; **built a custom free-space optical spectral analyser** combining APDs, coincidence counters, and wavelength filtering, with bespoke hardware and software.

**Result.** Characterised source spectra for visible and telecom bands and delivered a reusable single-photon measurement instrument — foundational to my later low-photon metrology work.
