**Problem.** Detect pollutant gases via changes in nanowire photoluminescence/UV absorption.

**Approach.** Studied InGaN nanowire UV-absorption spectra under pollutant gases; used **COMSOL** to model dependence on gas concentration, wire geometry, and spacing.

**Result.** Established geometry/spacing trends for sensitivity — an early grounding in optical device modelling.
