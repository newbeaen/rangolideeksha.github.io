**Problem.** Generate and control multi-wavelength light on an integrated InP platform for optical-to-THz signal processing.

**Approach.** Modelled and characterised the dynamical response of a multi-wavelength PIC laser; performed numerical/statistical modelling of nonlinear laser dynamics in MATLAB.

**Result.** Demonstrated optical-signal shifting by ~1.3 THz with high-speed switching (**10 Gb/s @ <1e-3 BER**).
