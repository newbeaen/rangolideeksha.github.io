**Problem.** Explore whether photonic quantum processors can accelerate reinforcement-learning routines.

**Approach.** At LOQCathon 2022 (Quandela), implemented a **quantum reinforcement-learning** algorithm for a nanophotonic switch on a quantum-circuit simulator and processor (PERCEVAL).

**Result.** Reproduced quantum-photonic speed-ups over classical baselines and proposed enhancements — team of 5.
