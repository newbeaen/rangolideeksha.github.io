**Problem.** Ising machines solve NP-hard optimisation by relaxing to a physical ground state, but convergence time and success rate limit their usefulness.

**Approach.** Implemented and benchmarked opto-electronic Ising-machine models in **Python** (time-to-solution, success rate) and explored a **Chaotic Amplitude Control** variant of the dynamics.

**Result.** Demonstrated an **~10× (orders-of-magnitude) improvement** in convergence time on NP-hard instances versus the baseline.
