**Problem.** Extract process metrics (waveguide loss, coupling) and de-risk designs via dedicated test structures on real foundry runs.

**Approach.** Designed ring-resonator and spiral-waveguide **test structures** on the Cornerstone silicon-photonic and AIM Photonics PDKs (GDSfactory/IPKISS); tracked die through fabrication and fibre packaging to connectorised parts.

**Result.** Delivered characterised test structures feeding waveguide-loss extraction and wafer statistics; completed AIM *Fabless PIC Design* and Cornerstone *PIC Bootcamp*.
