**Problem.** Warehouse AGV safety systems needed a robust, remotely powered controller certified for deployment across international sites.

**Approach.** Designed a **Power-over-Ethernet** controller integrating an ARM M4, Zigbee radio, switch-mode power supply, and sensors/actuators; led **EMI/EMC testing and CE certification** and owned lifecycle from spec to deployment.

**Result.** Deployed at multiple international sites; scalable for future additions.
