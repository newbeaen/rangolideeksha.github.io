**Problem.** Quantum-photonic devices need traceable, low-noise measurement across room-temperature and cryogenic regimes.

**Approach.** Developed precision metrology — traceable insertion-loss, >35 dB extinction-ratio, polarimetry, low-photon interferometry — and installed/commissioned probe station, vacuum and **helium-pumped SNSPD** cryogenic systems; ran the optics lab and vendor evaluation.

**Result.** A repeatable metrology stack feeding device design and yield decisions; lab and instrumentation built from the ground up.
