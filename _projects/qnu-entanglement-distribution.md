**Problem.** Distribute high-fidelity entanglement across a multi-node quantum networking unit (QNU) while meeting optical, RF, thermal and mechanical constraints.

**Approach.** Specified cross-domain requirements; developed Fock-space models linking system-level performance to component metrics; built the metrology and test benches to validate the chain.

**Result.** **>0.997-fidelity** entanglement distribution demonstrated across the networking unit.
