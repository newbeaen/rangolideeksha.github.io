**Problem.** On-chip optical filtering needs micro-ring resonators with well-controlled coupling and free-spectral-range — sensitive to sub-nm geometry and fabrication variation.

**Approach.** Designed and simulated coupled silicon-photonic micro-ring resonators for 1550 nm filtering using Python-based **IPKISS**; laid out the circuit and test structures, then characterised the fabricated devices.

**Result.** Working coupled-ring filters validated post-fabrication against design; built the design-to-measurement loop and the layout/test-structure discipline I now use across PIC tapeouts.

*Supervisor: Dr. W. Bogaerts, Ghent University. Tools: IPKISS, Python.*
