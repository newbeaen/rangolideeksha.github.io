**Problem.** Bring a thin-film lithium niobate (TFLN) switch network from design intent to validated, packaged silicon with tight loss and crosstalk budgets.

**Approach.** Modelled circuits in TFLN PDKs; defined cross-domain (optical/RF/thermal/mechanical) requirements; built the metrology for ultra-low-loss components, high-extinction switching, and photon-statistics validation across two tapeout generations.

**Result.** Packaged TFLN PIC at **<4 dB loss, >40 dB crosstalk**, bounded drift; ultra-low-loss components (<0.2 dB) and >0.997-fidelity entanglement distribution in the wider system.
